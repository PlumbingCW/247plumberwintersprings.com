Hydro jetting in Winter Springs, FL is the cleaning of a drain or sewer pipe with a high-pressure water jet that scours the full inside wall. A cable pokes a hole through a clog. A jet washes the whole pipe. Our dispatch runs 24/7 for lines that have stopped completely.

While you wait, stop using every drain in the house, including the washing machine and dishwasher. Every flush or load adds water to a line that has nowhere to go. Keep small children away from any overflow, since backed-up water carries bacteria.

My line was snaked and it clogged again. Why?

A cable cuts a channel through a blockage and pulls out part of it. The grease, soap and mineral film stuck to the pipe wall stays behind. That leftover coating narrows the pipe, and new debris catches on it within weeks.

This is the usual story behind a drain that is cleared every spring and backs up again by summer. The cable did its job. It was just the wrong tool for a line with coated walls.

Roots behave the same way. A cable chops the roots that have entered at a joint, but the stubs regrow from the same spot. Jetting clears the roots, scours the wall, and shows on camera where the pipe is open to the soil. If the pipe is cracked, we say so and discuss sewer line repair.

How a water jet clears a pipe

A jetting machine pumps water at high pressure through a hose with a specialized nozzle at the end. Water exits through angled ports. Some jets face backward, which pulls the hose forward through the pipe. Others spin or fan out and scour the wall.

Pressure is matched to the pipe. Cast iron and clay need a different setting than PVC. Too much pressure on a weak or cracked pipe can make damage worse, so a camera look before the jet matters.

Once the line is clear, we run the camera through it again. You see the clean pipe wall, and we note any cracks, offsets or sags that jetting revealed.

What happens on a jetting visit

The order matters. Skipping the camera is how pipes get damaged.

  1. 1

    Find the access point

    We locate a cleanout, or pull a toilet or open a vent if no cleanout exists.

  2. 2

    Camera the line

    A small camera shows the blockage, the pipe material and any cracks before water pressure goes in.

  3. 3

    Jet toward the clog

    We feed the hose in and work forward and back, clearing the plug first and then the wall.

  4. 4

    Camera again

    A second pass shows the pipe after cleaning, including defects the buildup was hiding.

  5. 5

    Explain what we saw

    We tell you whether the line is clean and sound, or whether it needs repair or a follow-up visit.

When a blocked sewer line is an emergency

Call right away when sewage is coming up in a tub or floor drain, when several fixtures back up together, or when water is reaching drywall or flooring. Those signs mean the main line is blocked.

Shut off water to the house at the main valve if overflow continues. Keep power away from wet areas, and do not run anything that drains. A full line cleaned with a jet is often the fastest way to restore service. For a plain main line clean-out, see sewer line cleaning.

Roots, grease and scale: what jetting removes best

Grease and soap scum respond well to hot water and a rotating nozzle, because the jet breaks the film off in sheets. Mineral scale takes more passes. Roots need a cutting nozzle in some cases, and heavy root masses may call for a cable first.

Jetting does not make a pipe new. It removes what has built up on the inside. If the pipe is thin, flaking or cracked, the camera will show it, and it is better to know than to keep paying for cleaning.

We do not offer chemical root killers as a substitute. They do not clean the wall, and they can harm other piping.

Commercial drains and trenchless questions

Restaurants and shops often have kitchen lines that clog with grease. Regular jetting on a schedule keeps them open, and we can quote a route for that. Commercial plumbing work gets the same camera check as a home.

Some people ask whether jetting is a trenchless method. It is not a repair. It is a cleaning step done before a trenchless liner is installed, because the liner needs a clean pipe wall. If the camera shows damage, trenchless repair becomes a separate conversation.

What shapes the cost of a jetting visit

You hear a clear price before work starts. These are the factors behind it.

Pipe size and length

A 2 inch kitchen branch is quicker than a 4 inch main drain that runs a long distance to the street.

Access

A cleanout makes the work simpler. Pulling a toilet or cutting in a cleanout adds time.

Severity of buildup

Light grease needs one pass. Heavy scale or roots need several.

Camera work

The before and after camera passes are part of the visit, and a longer recorded inspection costs more.

Hour of the call

Ask whether the hour of the call affects the price. You hear the price before work starts.

Insurance note

A sudden backup that ruins carpet is the kind of accidental damage insurance typically looks at. Scaled pipe, root intrusion and the jetting itself are wear and maintenance. Ask your insurer how your policy treats sewer backup.
