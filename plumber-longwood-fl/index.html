Before we arrive in Longwood

Backups and bursts both do more harm the longer they run. Start with these.

1

Shut the main valve

Turn the water main off if a pipe has burst or a fixture is flooding. If the problem is only a drain, shutting it off still limits what flows into the backup.

2

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for any room with standing water or sewage, only from a dry panel. Keep outlets and cords out of the wet area.

3

Clear the area

Stop running sinks, showers and the washing machine. Move rugs and boxes off the floor, and keep children and pets away from any sewage.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Sewer lines: find the failure before you dig

When several drains slow down at once, the problem is rarely at one sink. It sits in the main line, where roots, a sag or a cracked joint catch debris. A camera run shows which one, and measures how far from the house it sits.

Sewer line repair then depends on what the camera found. A short broken section can be replaced by itself. A long cracked run may suit cured-in-place lining, where a resin-soaked liner is cured inside the old pipe. Lining does not fix a pipe that has collapsed or has a belly that holds water.

We tell you which options fit before work starts.

Sewage in the house is a health issue. Keep people and pets out of the wet area, avoid touching it with bare hands and wash well afterward. We do not give medical advice, so call your doctor if someone feels ill.

Permits

Slow drains that come back

A single clog responds to a snake. A drain that keeps returning is different. Roots from nearby trees, grease buildup and bellied pipe all hold material between cleanings.

Drain cleaning with a cable clears the stoppage. If it returns within weeks, we recommend a camera look. Our sewer inspection work shows the cause on a screen before any digging, and it is a good idea before buying an older house.

A camera cannot see inside a slab, so we explain what it can and cannot show.
