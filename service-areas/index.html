Winter Springs itself

Our office is at 1160 E State Road 434, Winter Springs, FL 32708. The city is served through two ZIP codes, 32708 for street delivery and 32719 for PO boxes, and both are inside the service area.

We serve Tuscawilla, the Winter Springs Town Center area, the streets along Tuskawilla Road and the neighborhoods near Central Winds Park and Trotwood Park. The city runs its own water and wastewater utility, so for a problem on the city side of your meter the Winter Springs utility division can tell you where its responsibility ends.

What each town means for plumbing

Oviedo runs its own water system on Floridan wells with chloramine disinfection, which is rough on rubber seals in toilets and heaters. Casselberry is wrapped around dozens of lakes and ponds.

Longwood has a historic downtown and a city program that converts septic systems to sewer. Lake Mary includes the Heathrow area, and its city water is treated through carbon filtration. Sanford is the county seat on Lake Monroe, with its own two water treatment plants.

Distance matters less than the fault. A burst pipe or a sewage backup in Sanford gets the same priority as one in Winter Springs, and the plumber who answers will tell you the likely arrival window when you call. If you are not sure which town page fits your street, any of them will lead to the same line.

How we choose where to dispatch

Each town has its own page because the water, the housing and the calls differ from one place to the next. All five are within about ten miles of the office, so they share the same 24/7 emergency line and the same licensed plumbers, and no town is handled by a separate call center.

Outside those towns, call anyway. Orlando-area callers can ask whether a plumber can be sent, and we will give a straight answer on the phone instead of a form reply.

What helps us dispatch faster

Have the street address and the nearest cross street ready, and tell us which ZIP code you are in. Mention whether the problem is inside the house or outside near the meter, because that decides which plumber and which tools go first.

If you rent, tell us who owns the property and whether the manager has approved emergency calls, since that avoids a delay while we wait for permission. We can explain what we find to the property manager before any repair is approved.
