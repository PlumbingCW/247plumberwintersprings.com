House, townhome or condo: who fixes what

In a detached house, everything from the meter inward is yours. In an attached townhome or a condo, the line between your pipe and the building's pipe is set by your association's documents, not by a plumber.

A rule of thumb helps while you read them. A fixture, valve or drain that serves only your unit is usually yours. A drain stack or supply line that serves several units is often the association's. If water is reaching a neighbor, tell your association or manager as well as calling us.

A city water plant on Sheoah Boulevard

The City of Winter Springs lists three water plants, and one sits on Sheoah Boulevard behind the Highlands tennis courts. The city draws groundwater from the Floridan Aquifer and treats it by aeration and liquid chlorine.

The city publishes hardness as one average for its whole system, 150 mg/L or 8 grains, so the figure does not change by neighborhood in its data. Scale on a tank heater is a citywide reason to flush it and to ask about a water softener.

Older homes and shutoff valves nobody has turned

Listing data suggests many Highlands homes date from the 1970s into the mid 1980s. That is not a defect. It does mean most water heaters have been replaced at least once, and valves under sinks and behind toilets may not have moved in years.

Turn each shutoff gently once a year so it works on the day you need it. A valve that will not close, or one that weeps when you try, is a small repair worth doing before a leak, and it is a normal call for plumbing installation work.
