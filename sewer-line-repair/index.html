Sewer line repair in Winter Springs, FL means fixing a section of the pipe that carries wastewater from your home to the street connection, once it has cracked, separated, sagged or been invaded by roots. If wastewater is backing up now, our 24/7 dispatch can send a licensed plumber. Stop using water and drains while you wait.

Do not run the washer or flush the toilet to see if it clears. Each use sends more water into a pipe that has already failed, and it can spread contamination across the floor.

Spot dig, liner or replacement: choosing the repair

The right repair depends on what the camera finds. This is how the main choices compare.

RepairBest forLimits
Spot repair by diggingOne cracked or offset joint, or a short root-damaged sectionNeeds an excavation and restoration of the surface above
Pipe lining (cured-in-place)Long runs with several cracks or roots at jointsNeeds a clear, round pipe, and cannot fix a collapsed or badly offset section
Full replacement, open trenchA pipe that has collapsed or sags along a long stretchMost disruptive to landscaping and driveways

A camera cannot see inside a slab, but it can show the condition of the pipe where it can travel.

Spot repair when roots have broken in

Roots enter a pipe through a joint or a small crack in search of water. Once inside, they thicken and widen the opening, and soil follows. A cable can clear the roots, but they return until the opening is sealed.

A spot repair removes the damaged length and replaces it with new pipe and couplings. We locate it with a camera and a locator, so the excavation is small and only as long as the break.

After the repair we run the camera through again to confirm the line is clear and the joints are tight. Roots that remain in nearby sections are cleaned out with hydro jetting before the line is put back in service.

When a sewer backup means the pipe has failed

A backup caused by a failed pipe looks like any other backup. The difference is that cleaning only helps for a while. If a line has been cleared several times and keeps stopping, the pipe is likely damaged.

We begin with the same steps as sewer line cleaning to restore flow, then scope the line to find out why it stopped. That way the emergency is handled now and the repair is planned from facts.

If the line has collapsed and flow cannot be restored, we tell you that plainly and discuss options for getting a working connection back.

How cured-in-place pipe lining works

Lining is also called CIPP. It builds a new pipe inside the old one, with no trench across the yard.

  1. 1

    Clean the pipe

    Roots and scale come out first, since the liner needs a clean wall.

  2. 2

    Soak the liner

    A felt tube is saturated with epoxy resin sized for the pipe diameter.

  3. 3

    Pull and inflate it

    The tube is drawn into place and inflated so it presses against the pipe wall.

  4. 4

    Cure it

    Hot water, steam or light hardens the resin into a smooth, rigid pipe.

  5. 5

    Reopen connections

    Branch openings are cut with a robot, and the finished line is cameraed to confirm.

Lateral replacement, trenchless methods and lining under a slab

A lateral is the line that connects the house to the sewer main. When it is badly damaged, replacement can be done by open trench, or by trenchless methods such as pipe bursting, which pulls a new pipe through the old one.

Lining can also help where a pipe runs under a slab and cannot be dug without breaking concrete. An epoxy liner can seal cracks without opening the floor, though the pipe has to be clean and round for it to work.

Not every line suits a trenchless method. We tell you what the camera shows and what each method can and cannot do.

Gravity mains, force mains and permits in Winter Springs

The city's utility division lists 112 miles of gravity sewer mains, 24 miles of force mains and 43 lift stations. Most houses drain by gravity, but a lift station pushes wastewater through a pressurized force main when the ground does not allow gravity flow. Where your lateral meets the system is a question for the city Utility Division, which can tell you where city responsibility ends.

A sewer repair may need a permit, and permit rules vary, so ask the Winter Springs building department. The city's permit application says a separate permit must be secured for plumbing work, and it asks for a contractor license number. You can also check any plumber with the Florida DBPR Verify a Licensee search.

If you have a septic system

This page is about the sewer line that connects to a city system. If your property has a septic tank, the drain field and the tank are a different kind of job, and not every blockage there is a pipe problem.

A septic tank needs pumping and inspection by a company that does that work. We can check the pipe between the house and the tank with a camera. We do not pump tanks or repair drain fields, and we say so if that is what the problem turns out to be.

What drives the price of a sewer repair

We give a clear price before work starts. Sewer work varies widely, and these are the main reasons.

Depth of the pipe

A shallow pipe is cheaper to reach than one several feet down.

Length of damage

One bad joint is a short dig. A long cracked run points toward lining or replacement.

What is on top

Lawn is simpler to restore than a driveway, a patio or a tree root zone.

Repair method

Lining, bursting and open trench use different crews, materials and time.

Permits and inspection

Permit rules vary, so ask the Winter Springs building department what applies to your job.

Insurance note

If a collapsed line suddenly floods the house, insurance typically looks at that accidental water damage. Wear, root intrusion and the cost of repairing the failed pipe itself are usually treated differently. Ask your insurer about sewer line coverage.
